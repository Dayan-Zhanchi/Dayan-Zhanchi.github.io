Research Interests

I have a general interest in generative models, including GANs, energy-based models, score-based models, normalizing flows, and diffusion models. I am particularly interested in exploring the connections between different diffusion paradigms.
My current work focuses on improving consistency in diffusion models, especially for text-to-image generation. I am also exploring how to make world models efficient enough to run on consumer-grade GPUs.
